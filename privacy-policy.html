Who we are

MY SPORTS NETWORK ("we," "us," "the app") is a sports companion app currently focused on Golf, with more sports planned. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your email address and password (your password is never stored in readable form — it's securely handled by our authentication provider, Supabase)
  • Your display name
  • A profile photo, if you choose to upload one (entirely optional)
  • A title (like "Coach" or "Athlete"), if you choose to add one

As you use the app, we also store:

  • Which sport you're following (your "home sport"), and which sports you've explored
  • Activity like poll votes and reward points earned, tracked separately for each sport
  • Content you post (if you're a verified account) in the Pro Voice section, including text, photos, or voice recordings you choose to share
  • Which posts you've reacted to

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone
  • We do not share your data with advertisers
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites

Third-party services we use

To run the app, we rely on a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles secure sign-in
  • Vercel — hosts the app itself
  • Google Gemini — used only by our internal team to help write news summaries from public articles; this does not involve your personal account data

Your controls

  • You can edit your display name, title, and photo at any time from your profile
  • You can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly within the app, at any time, from the profile screen

Who can see what

Your email and account settings are private to you. If you're a verified Pro account, your public posts (name, title, and content) are visible to anyone using the app for that sport, similar to a public social media post. Regular member profiles are not visible to other members.

Children

This app is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
